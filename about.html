MILESTONE TIMELINE

Corporate Evolution (1966 – 2019)

Key dates tracing our growth from a partnership firm to a globally recognized technical textile venture.

1966

Inception

Originally constituted as a partnership firm, reconstituted over time into a pioneering industrial force.

1993 - 1994

Naviska Packaging Private Limited

Incorporated in 1993. Started PP Woven SA/HDPE sacks at DTA Pithampur (1,380 MT initial capacity).

2001 - 2002

Giltpac International

Re-named Giltpac International India Private Limited w.e.f. June 29, 2001 to drive export expansion.

2003 - 2004

Flexituff International & SEZ Launch

Commenced FIBC production at SEZ Pithampur (7,800 MT). Converted status to Public Limited as Flexituff International Limited.

2005 - 2006

BRC & AIB Cleanroom Certifications

Expanded DTA capacity to 5,200 MT. Received BRC & AIB certifications for food-grade cleanroom packaging.

2007 - 2008

SEZ R&D Unit & PE Funding

Set up 3 dedicated R&D labs. Received Rs 460 mn PE investment from Clearwater Capital Partners. Combined capacity reached 23,000 MT.

2009 - 2010

Pithampur 16,000 MT Facility

Commissioned 16,000 MT plant expansion for technical woven & Geo-Textile fabrics.

2011 - 2012

Successful IPO (Rs 1,046 MN)

Flexituff went public with a landmark Rs 1,046 million IPO and bagged a Rs 550 mn mega order for Geo-Bags.

2013 - 2014

TPG / IFC Funding & Corporate Award

Strategic PE investment by TPG & IFC. Won ET Bengal Corporate Award as the Fastest Growing Company in India.

2015 - 2016

India's Largest Non-Woven Geo-Plant

Commissioned 10,300 MT Non-Woven Geo-Textile plant. Named in Dun & Bradstreet Top 500 Companies in India.

2017 - 2018

Stitchless Fusion Bag Rights

Developed cutting-edge needle-free stitchless bag technology and exclusive manufacturing rights for fusion FIBCs.

2018 - 2019

Rebranding to Flexituff Technology

Re-branded to Flexituff Technology International Limited to reflect multi-product venture leadership across FIBC, Technical Textiles, and Infrastructure.
