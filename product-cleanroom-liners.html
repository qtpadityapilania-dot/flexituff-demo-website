Product Overview

For high-purity food, pharmaceutical, and moisture-sensitive chemicals, Flexituff produces specialty liners inside ISO Class 7 cleanroom facilities. Featuring ultrasonic cutting, light table inspection, and hermetic sealing to provide complete barrier protection against oxygen, moisture, and contamination.

Technical Specifications

Cleanroom RatingISO 14644-1 Class 7 Certified Facility
Liner VarietiesForm-Fit, Aluminium Foil Barrier, EVOH Oxygen Barrier, Nylon, Conductive
Attachment OptionsTabbed, Glued, Flanged, or Suspended Liners
Regulatory ComplianceFDA 21 CFR, EU 10/2011, BRCGS AA Certified

Key Design Features & Advantages

Zero Contamination

Manufactured under positive pressure cleanroom environment with 100% light table inspection.

High Barrier Shielding

Aluminium & EVOH multi-layer films prevent moisture ingress and oxygen oxidation.

Form-Fit Precision

Engineered to contour exact interior dimensions of the bulk bag with zero liner creasing.

Hermetic Sealing

Leak-proof ultrasonic seams prevent volatile gas or aroma loss.
