Product Overview

Flexituff holds global patents for the FlexiTunnel® Lift design. Engineered specifically for building sites and building merchants, side fabric tunnels allow forklift tines to slide directly into the sides of the bag without needing manual loop attachment.

Key Design Features & Advantages

Single-Operator Forklift Handling

Forklift driver inserts tines directly into side tunnels without leaving the vehicle cab.

Reduced Site Labor

Speeds up loading and unloading at construction sites and building merchant yards.

Heavy-Duty Construction

Reinforced fabric walls withstand abrasive aggregates, sand, and rubble.
