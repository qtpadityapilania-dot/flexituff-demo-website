Standard Bulk Bags

Type A — Standard Polypropylene FIBC

Type A FIBCs are made from plain woven polypropylene without any electrostatic protection. They are the most common FIBC type and are designed exclusively for non-flammable dry goods where no static hazard exists in the surrounding atmosphere.

Warning: Do NOT use Type A bags in the presence of flammable gases, solvents, or combustible dust. There is no static protection whatsoever.
Static ProtectionNone
Grounding RequiredNot applicable
Breakdown VoltageNo restriction
SWL Range500 kg – 2,000 kg
Best ApplicationsSand, gravel, stone, grain, non-flammable minerals
StandardIEC 61340-4-4
<6kV Breakdown Voltage

Type B — Anti-Static Breakdown Protection

Type B FIBCs are constructed from polypropylene fabric with a thin insulating coating or specific weave that prevents high-energy propagating brush discharges (PBD). Rigorously tested under IEC 61340-4-4 to guarantee breakdown voltage stays strictly below 6kV.

Caution: Type B bags protect against propagating brush discharges but do NOT dissipate static charge. They must NOT be used in flammable gas or vapor atmospheres.
Breakdown VoltageStrictly < 6kV
Grounding RequiredNot required
Protects AgainstPropagating Brush Discharge (PBD)
SWL Range500 kg – 2,000 kg
Best ApplicationsDry combustible powders with MIE > 3 mJ (starch, sugar, resin)
StandardIEC 61340-4-4
Resistance < 10&sup8; Ohms

Type C — Groundable Conductive FIBC

Type C FIBCs feature an interconnected network of conductive carbon grid threads woven throughout the bag body and lifting loops. All conductive components must be grounded via a grounding cable during every fill and discharge operation to safely drain electrostatic charge to earth.

Safe for: Explosive powder environments and flammable solvent atmospheres — only when properly grounded to a verified earthing point.
Resistance to Ground< 10&sup8; Ohms
Grounding RequiredMANDATORY operator cable
Dissipation MethodConductive thread grid to earth
SWL Range500 kg – 2,000 kg
Best ApplicationsExplosive powders, pharma APIs, chemical solvents
StandardIEC 61340-4-4
Official Crohmiq® Licensee

Type D — Crohmiq® Static Dissipative

Flexituff is the exclusive Indian licensee of Texene USA, producing patented Crohmiq® Type D static dissipative bulk bags. Engineered with quasi-conductive threads that safely dissipate static charges into the atmosphere via low-energy, harmless corona discharge — completely eliminating the risk of human error with grounding cables.

No Grounding Required: Type D Crohmiq® bags are safe for Zone 1, Zone 2, Zone 21, and Zone 22 hazardous locations without any operator grounding connection.
Dissipation MethodLow-energy Corona Discharge into air
Grounding RequiredZERO — Fail-safe design
LicensingOfficial Texene Crohmiq® USA Licensee
SWL Range500 kg – 2,000 kg
Zone SafetyZone 0, 1, 2, 21, 22 Hazardous Locations
StandardIEC 61340-4-4 & BS EN 13432
